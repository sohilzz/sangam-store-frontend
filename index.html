About the shop

Sangam Store is a trusted supplier of sewing and stitching machines, serving tailoring businesses , garment manufacturers, dealers and individual customers. We offer a range of industrial and domestic sewing machines from reliable brands, along with essential accessories and spare parts.

Sangam Store has been supplying industrial and domestic stitching machines to tailoring businesses for over 25 years. We stock leading brands alongside our own reconditioned units, and every machine leaving our shop is tested on the floor before it goes out.

We work directly with tailoring units, garment factories and retail dealers, offering wholesale pricing, bulk supply and machine servicing under one roof.
